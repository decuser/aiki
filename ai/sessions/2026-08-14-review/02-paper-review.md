# 02 — MISQ rejection assessment

Status: **GATED**

## Intent

Assess the EIC rejection of manuscript 2026-TR-21387, "Beyond Effective Use:
AI and the Failure of Productive Stability," submitted to MIS Quarterly as
Theory and Review.

## Letter assessment

The EIC (Susan Brown) placed the paper in the Theory Development category
("challenging an existing foundation") and then prescribed systematic review,
which is the method that defines the other TR category (Theory-Generative
Research Synthesis). This is an internal contradiction checkable against her
own paragraph.

The "strawman" charge is misapplied. The paper engages TAM, UTAUT, DeLone &
McLean, TTF, and effective use theory — the IS evaluation canon. Selecting
the field's most-cited formulations is the opposite of constructing a weak
opponent.

Decision is EIC-only, no referees, marked final. This is structurally a desk
reject.

## Manuscript assessment

After reading the full manuscript, one substantive finding emerged that the
letter pointed toward without articulating clearly:

**Capability concealment (Section 7) is asserted, not evidenced.** This is
the paper's most consequential organizational claim. No case, no data, no
instance of an organization in the concealment state. It is presented as a
discovered failure mode but it is a deduction from the constitutive/erosive
distinction. The strongest workplace-scale study in the paper (Brynjolfsson
et al. 2025) found learning that persisted when AI was unavailable — a
counterexample to the erosion mechanism at the load-bearing point.

**K ≠ K′ depends on a single self-citation.** The formal criterion doing the
most work is sourced to Senn 2026 (advance online, Journal of
Documentation). "The central point is reconstructed here" is a paragraph. At
MISQ this reads as an unsubstantiated self-citation at the spine of the
argument.

**The format charge has teeth despite the category error.** No systematic
accounting of the IS literature on IT and skill, automation complacency,
learning-by-doing, or absorptive capacity. The productive-stability reading
of the five evaluation frameworks is asserted as characterization, never
demonstrated from their texts. This is the demonstrable version of the EIC's
complaint, accessed through the wrong category's requirements.

## Recommendations

- Keep Theory Development category; do not concede the systematic-review
  frame.
- Show the productive-stability reading from the framework texts rather than
  asserting it.
- Either evidence capability concealment with case data or demote it from
  finding to proposition.
- Make the K ≠ K′ criterion self-contained in this paper or hedge the
  self-citation dependency.
- Consider venue: JAIS, Information and Organization, or a debates/
  perspectives format may metabolize the argument form better than MISQ's
  TR category.
